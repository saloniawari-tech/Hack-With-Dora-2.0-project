RENTAL AGREEMENT

Clause 1:
The tenant shall pay monthly rent of INR 20000 before the 5th day of every month.

Clause 2:
The tenant must provide a 90 day notice before leaving the property.

Clause 3:
The landlord may deduct 20% of the security deposit for repainting charges.

Clause 4:
The tenant must maintain all appliances and repair costs will be paid by the tenant.

Clause 5:
The tenant agrees to a lock-in period of 12 months.

Clause 6:
The landlord can increase rent at any time during the agreement.

Clause 7:
Late payment will result in a penalty of INR 5000.

Clause 8:
Electricity and water charges must be paid separately by the tenant.
