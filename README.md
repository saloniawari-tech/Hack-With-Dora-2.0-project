# 🛡️ Renter Shield

### AI-Powered Rental Agreement Risk Analyzer

Renter Shield is an AI-powered application that helps tenants understand rental agreements before signing them.

Rental agreements can contain complicated legal language, hidden fees, strict notice periods, security deposit deductions, lock-in periods, and other clauses that tenants may overlook.

Renter Shield uses AI to analyze rental agreements and identify potentially risky clauses in simple, easy-to-understand language.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Many tenants sign rental agreements without fully understanding the terms and conditions.

Common problems include:

- Hidden maintenance charges
- Strict notice periods
- Security deposit deductions
- Lock-in periods
- Early termination penalties
- Late payment fees
- Unclear rent-increase clauses
- Complicated legal language

Most tenants don't have the time or legal knowledge to carefully analyze every clause.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Renter Shield allows users to upload a rental agreement and receive an AI-powered analysis.

The system identifies potentially unfavorable clauses and presents them using a simple **Risk Scorecard**.
